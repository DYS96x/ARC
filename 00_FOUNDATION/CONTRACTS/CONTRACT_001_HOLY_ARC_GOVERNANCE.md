# CONTRACT_001 — HOLY ARC Governance Contract

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Draft  
Authority: HOLY ARC Constitution  
Classification: Internal

---

# Contract

> HOLY ARC provides constitutional governance for the ecosystem while remaining separated from operational execution.

---

# Purpose

Define the relationship between HOLY ARC and all ARC systems.

This contract protects:

- Constitutional authority
- System independence
- Responsibility boundaries
- Decision integrity

---

# HOLY ARC Responsibility

HOLY ARC owns:

- Constitution
- Laws
- Standards
- Approved methodology
- Governance rules
- Constitutional amendments
- Architectural principles

---

# HOLY ARC Does Not Own

HOLY ARC does not own:

- Daily execution
- Client operations
- Workflow state
- Platform runtime
- Implementation details

---

# Governance Interface

Systems may request:

- Constitutional review
- Rule interpretation
- Amendment consideration
- Architecture alignment review

---

# Required Inputs

Requests to HOLY ARC should include:

- Request purpose
- Affected systems
- Evidence
- Current state
- Proposed change
- Impact assessment

---

# HOLY ARC Outputs

HOLY ARC may provide:

- Approval
- Rejection
- Guidance
- Constitutional interpretation
- Required amendments

---

# Authority Boundary

HOLY ARC may:

✅ Define rules

✅ Review alignment

✅ Approve constitutional changes

✅ Preserve history


HOLY ARC may not:

❌ Execute operations

❌ Modify systems directly

❌ Replace system ownership

❌ Declare truth without evidence

---

# Relationship Model

```text
HOLY ARC

Governance

⇅

Systems

Execution
```

Communication is bidirectional.

Authority remains separated.

---

# Change Rules

Changes affecting constitutional authority require:

- Evidence
- Review
- Approval
- Amendment record

---

# Failure Handling

If a system violates this contract:

```text
Detect

↓

Record

↓

Investigate

↓

Resolve

↓

Learn
```

---

# Validation Criteria

This contract is satisfied when:

- Governance remains separate from execution.
- Authority is clear.
- Decisions are traceable.
- Systems remain accountable.

---

# Final Principle

> Governance protects the direction of the ecosystem. It does not drive every movement.
