# CONTRACT_010 — Evolution Contract

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Draft  
Authority: HOLY ARC Standards  
Classification: Internal

---

# Contract

> ARC systems shall evolve through evidence, feedback and controlled change while preserving constitutional integrity, historical continuity and responsibility boundaries.

---

# Purpose

Define how ARC changes over time.

Evolution exists to ensure:

- Systems improve.
- Knowledge compounds.
- Architecture remains adaptable.
- History is preserved.
- Reality remains the final authority.

---

# Evolution Principle

ARC does not resist change.

ARC learns from change.

HOLY ARC governs constitutional change.

```text
Reality

↓

Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Learning

↓

Change

↓

Validation

↓

Improved System
```

- Standard changes

# Change Classification

Changes are classified as:

---

## Patch Change

Small clarification.

Examples:

- Documentation updates
- Wording improvements
- Non-functional corrections

---

## Minor Change

Capability improvement.

Examples:

- New functionality
- New workflow capability
- Improved process

---

## Major Change

Structural evolution.

Examples:

- New responsibility
- Changed ownership
- Constitutional impact
- Architectural redesign

---


---

# Preservation Rules

Evolution must preserve:

- Identity
- Ownership
- History
- Provenance
- Accountability

Change does not erase the past.

Change builds upon it.

---

# Feedback Loop

ARC evolution follows:

```text
Observe

↓

Challenge

↓

Understand

↓

Improve

↓

Measure

↓

Learn
```

---

# Reality Validation

Every change must eventually answer:

- Did reality improve?
- Did capability increase?
- Did complexity reduce?
- Did trust increase?
- Did knowledge compound?

---

# Evolution Boundaries

Evolution must not:

- Remove accountability.
- Hide history.
- Bypass governance.
- Create unclear ownership.
- Convert assumptions into facts.

---

# Architecture Evolution

Architecture should evolve when:

- Reality requires change.
- Evidence supports improvement.
- Current design limits capability.

Architecture should not change because of:

- Trend chasing.
- Temporary pressure.
- Unvalidated assumptions.

---

# Knowledge Evolution

Every completed evolution should create:

- New knowledge.
- Updated patterns.
- Improved methodology.
- Better future decisions.

---

# Quality Criteria

This contract is satisfied when:

- Change is intentional.
- Evidence supports evolution.
- History remains intact.
- Responsibility remains clear.
- Future capability increases.

---

# Violations

The following violate this contract:

- Silent structural changes.
- Removing history.
- Changing ownership without governance.
- Implementing without evidence.
- Optimising short-term outcomes at long-term cost.

---

# Final Principle

> The strongest systems are not those that never change. They are systems that learn how to change correctly.
