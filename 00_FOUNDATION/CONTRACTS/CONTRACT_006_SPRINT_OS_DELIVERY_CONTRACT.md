# CONTRACT_006 — Sprint OS Delivery Contract

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Draft  
Authority: HOLY ARC Standards  
Classification: Internal

---

# Contract

> Sprint OS provides the controlled improvement capability of the ecosystem by converting approved priorities into measurable delivery cycles.

---

# Purpose

Define the relationship between Sprint OS and systems requiring improvement execution.

Sprint OS exists to create:

- Focused improvement
- Measurable progress
- Controlled delivery
- Learning cycles
- Capability growth

---

# Sprint OS Responsibility

Sprint OS owns:

- Sprint definition
- Sprint objectives
- Improvement goals
- Task coordination
- Dependencies
- Reviews
- Measurements
- Delivery evidence
- Lessons learned

---

# Sprint OS Does Not Own

Sprint OS does not own:

- Organisational diagnosis
- Constitutional authority
- Permanent memory
- Final business decisions
- System execution infrastructure

---

# Sprint Model

Sprint OS follows:

```text
Approved Improvement

↓

Sprint Goal

↓

Plan

↓

Execute

↓

Measure

↓

Review

↓

↓

Improve
```

```text

Duration

Evidence

```

---

# Improvement Principles

## I001 — System Before Task

Tasks exist to improve systems.

Activity alone does not equal progress.

---

## I002 — Smallest Complete Improvement

Sprints should deliver meaningful capability.

Avoid incomplete fragments.

---

## I003 — Measure Before Declaring Success

Every sprint requires evidence.

---

## I004 — Learning Is Mandatory

Every sprint produces:

- Lessons
- Knowledge
- Process improvements
- Future recommendations

# Sprint Review

Every completed sprint requires:

## Outcome Review

What changed?

## Evidence Review

What proves improvement?

## Learning Review

What should continue?

## Multiplier Review

Did future capability increase?

---

# Sprint Quality Criteria

This contract is satisfied when:

- Goals are clear.
- Ownership exists.
- Progress is measurable.
- Evidence is captured.
- Learning is preserved.
- Future capability improves.

---

# Violations

The following violate this contract:

- Sprints without goals.
- Tasks without purpose.
- Completion without evidence.
- No lessons recorded.
- Executing before approval.
- Confusing activity with improvement.

---

# Final Principle

> Sprint OS turns understanding into improvement through focused, measurable evolution.
