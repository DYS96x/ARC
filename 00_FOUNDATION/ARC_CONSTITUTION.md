Status: Foundational

# Preamble

ARC exists to design systems that improve how people, organisations and intelligence work together.

Technology changes.

Markets change.

Tools change.

Principles endure.

This Constitution establishes the permanent foundations upon which every ARC decision, platform, methodology, product and relationship is built.

Every future document derives its authority from this Constitution.
