# STD_005 — Knowledge

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Draft  
Authority: HOLY ARC Laws  
Classification: Internal

---

# Standard

> Every ARC knowledge object shall preserve context, evidence, confidence, relationships and history so that understanding can compound over time.

Information is stored.

Knowledge is understood.

---

# Purpose

Define how ARC creates, validates, preserves and evolves knowledge.

This standard ensures knowledge remains:

- Connected
- Traceable
- Contextual
- Validated
- Reusable
- Continuously improving

---

# Relationship To Laws

This standard derives from:

## LAW 001 — Responsibility

Knowledge requires ownership.

## LAW 002 — Evidence

Knowledge must maintain connection to supporting evidence.

## LAW 004 — Truth

Knowledge must distinguish facts, assumptions and validated understanding.

## LAW 005 — State

Knowledge is separate from temporary operational state.

## LAW 007 — Change

Knowledge evolves through controlled learning.

---

# Knowledge Definition

Knowledge is validated understanding derived from:

- Evidence
- Relationships
- Context
- Experience
- Outcomes
- Reflection

Knowledge is not simply accumulated information.

---

# Knowledge Model

ARC knowledge follows:

```text
Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Assessment

↓

Understanding

↓

Knowledge

↓

Pattern

↓

Principle

↓

Improved Action

↓

New Evidence
```

Knowledge is part of a continuous learning cycle.

---

# Knowledge Classification

Every knowledge object should identify its type.

---

## Fact

A confirmed statement supported by evidence.

---

## Observation

A recorded perception or measurement.

---

## Assumption

An unvalidated belief requiring investigation.

---

## Hypothesis

A proposed explanation requiring testing.

---

## Pattern

A repeated relationship observed across contexts.

---

## Principle

A validated rule or guiding concept.

---

## Constitutional Knowledge

Knowledge formally accepted into ARC governance.

---

# Knowledge Requirements

Every knowledge object should contain:

## Identity

- Unique identifier
- Type
- Version

---

## Context

- Origin
- Purpose
- Environment
- Relevant relationships

---

## Evidence

- Supporting sources
- Confidence
- Validation status

---

## Ownership

- Responsible owner
- Review responsibility

---

## History

- Creation
- Changes
- Previous versions
- Learning events

---

# Knowledge Relationships

Knowledge should connect to:

- Evidence
- Systems
- Objects
- Decisions
- Outcomes
- Other knowledge

Disconnected knowledge loses value.

---

# Knowledge Confidence

Knowledge shall represent confidence.

Confidence may consider:

- Evidence strength
- Source reliability
- Validation level
- Recurrence
- Contradictory information

Confidence must remain visible.

---

# Knowledge Evolution

Knowledge improves through:

```text
New Evidence

↓

Review

↓

Validation

↓

Update

↓

Learning
```

Previous understanding should be preserved.

Learning replaces ignorance.

It does not erase history.

---

# Knowledge Governance

Important knowledge requires:

- Owner
- Review process
- Version control
- Access rules
- Retirement criteria

---

# Knowledge Quality Tests

Knowledge passes this standard when:

- Meaning is clear.
- Evidence is connected.
- Confidence is visible.
- Ownership exists.
- History is preserved.
- Relationships are maintained.
- Learning can compound.

---

# Violations

The following violate this standard:

- Knowledge without evidence.
- Facts mixed with assumptions.
- Lost context.
- No ownership.
- Silent modification.
- Removing historical understanding.
- Treating information storage as knowledge.
