# SPEC_007 — Governance Architecture

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Draft  
Authority: HOLY ARC Standards  
Classification: Internal

---

# Specification

> Every ARC system shall operate within a governed decision framework that preserves authority, evidence, accountability and historical continuity.

Governance transforms decisions into trusted action.

---

# Purpose

Define the architecture required for constitutional governance.

Governance provides:

- Decision control
- Authority management
- Review processes
- Auditability
- Constitutional alignment

---

HOLY ARC governs long-term ecosystem evolution.

## LAW 002 — Evidence

Decisions remain challengeable by reality.

```text

↓

Evidence

↓

Assessment

↓

Decision

↓

Approval

↓

Execution

↓

Outcome

↓

Review

↓

Learning
```

---

# Governance Responsibilities

## HOLY ARC

Owns:

- Constitutional authority
- Laws
- Standards
- Amendments
- Governance rules

Does not own:

- Daily operations
- Implementation
- Execution

---

## System Owners

Own:

- System decisions
- Performance
- Improvement
- Risk management

---

## Operators

Own:

- Approved execution
- Operational outcomes
- Reporting

---

```text
Decision ID

Purpose

Owner

Evidence

Options Considered

Decision

Approval

Outcome
```

---

# Decision Categories

## Operational Decision

Affects daily execution.

Owner:

System responsible for execution.

---

## Architectural Decision

Affects system design.

Requires:

- Evidence
- Impact analysis
- Review

---

## Constitutional Decision

Affects:

- Laws
- Principles
- Authority

Requires HOLY ARC governance.

---

# Approval Model

Approval requires:

- Appropriate authority
- Supporting evidence
- Defined scope
- Recorded decision

Approval cannot exceed constitutional authority.

---

# Review Model

Governance reviews should evaluate:

## Alignment

Does the decision follow constitutional rules?

## Evidence

Was evidence sufficient?

## Impact

What systems and relationships are affected?

## Outcome

Did reality validate the decision?

---

# Challenge Process

Valid challenges require:

- Decision reference
- Evidence
- Constitutional concern
- Proposed resolution

Challenges create improvement opportunities.

---

# Audit Trail

Governance records shall preserve:

- Decision history
- Approvals
- Changes
- Reviews
- Outcomes

History cannot be silently removed.

---

# Governance State

Governance maintains:

```text
Pending

↓

Reviewed

↓

Approved

↓

Active

↓

Reviewed

↓

Archived
```

---

# Governance Security

Governance actions require:

- Identity
- Authority
- Permission
- Evidence

---

# Governance Quality Tests

Governance passes when:

- Authority is clear.
- Decisions are recorded.
- Evidence exists.
- Reviews occur.
- History is preserved.
- Accountability remains.

---

# Violations

The following violate this specification:

- Decisions without owners.
- Approval without authority.
- Hidden governance changes.
- Missing evidence.
- Deleted history.
- Execution outside approved scope.
