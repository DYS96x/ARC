# Purpose

This Constitution defines the identity, purpose, principles, responsibilities, boundaries, decision rules and evolution process of the DYS96 ecosystem.

It exists to preserve coherence while allowing validated evolution.

The Constitution does not define reality.

Reality remains the final authority.

---

# Constitutional Role of HOLY ARC

HOLY ARC is the guardian of the Constitution.

HOLY ARC governs the long-term evolution of the ecosystem by:

- Preserving constitutional integrity
- Maintaining approved methodology
- Maintaining architectural standards
- Reviewing evidence
- Recording constitutional amendments
- Preventing responsibility drift
- Preserving constitutional memory

HOLY ARC does not execute operational work.

HOLY ARC does not own client data.

HOLY ARC does not declare truth without evidence.

---

# Ecosystem Definition

The DYS96 ecosystem is a living network of independent systems that exchange information, resources, decisions and feedback to achieve shared purposes while continuously adapting to reality.

The ecosystem is not a one-way pipeline.

Responsibilities are stable.

Communication is adaptive.

Every meaningful relationship should support appropriate feedback.

---

# System Definition

A system is an autonomous capability with:

- A defined purpose
- Clear responsibilities
- Explicit boundaries
- Measurable outcomes
- Governed relationships
- Evidence of performance
- A process for adaptation

A system must not silently assume the primary responsibility of another system.

---

# Relationship Definition

A relationship is a governed interaction between two or more constitutional objects or systems.

A relationship may carry:

- Information
- Evidence
- Decisions
- Resources
- Permissions
- Constraints
- Risk
- Context
- Feedback
- History

Relationships are first-class constitutional objects.

Systems create capability.

Relationships create the ecosystem.

---

# Flow Definition

Flow is the movement of information, decisions, resources or feedback through governed relationships according to the current state of the ecosystem.

Flow does not require a universal beginning or ending.

Flow is adaptive, state-dependent and capable of feedback.

# Constitutional Objects

The ecosystem recognises the following constitutional objects.

## Entity

Something that exists independently of a specific body of work.

Examples:

- Company
- Person
- Product
- Team
- Department
- Organisation
- Platform

## Engagement

A purposeful body of work connected to one or more entities.

Examples:

- Company health check
- Full company analysis
- Investigation
- Sprint
- Research project
- Product build
- Transformation programme

## Artifact

A persistent output or evidence object.

Examples:

- Report
- Document
- Interview record
- Source code
- Image
- Diagram
- Dataset
- Audit log
- Decision record

## Knowledge

Validated understanding derived from evidence, relationships and outcomes.

Knowledge must distinguish between:

- Confirmed fact
- Organisational statement
- ARC observation
- Assumption
- Unknown
- Validated pattern
- Constitutional knowledge

## Relationship

A governed connection between constitutional objects.

## History

A persistent record of change over time.

# Constitutional Laws

## Law 01 — Reality Is the Final Authority

Every model, score, prediction, recommendation, architecture and constitutional assumption remains challengeable by validated evidence.

## Law 02 — Meaning Before Storage

The ecosystem is organised by constitutional meaning, not by files, folders or persistence technology.

## Law 03 — Context Before Navigation

Users should resume work from restored context rather than reconstructing it through folders and files.

## Law 04 — Relationships Are First-Class

Relationships are governed constitutional objects with purpose, participants, permissions, constraints, evidence, feedback and history.

## Law 05 — One Canonical Owner

Every constitutional concept has one canonical owner and one authoritative source.

## Law 06 — Responsibility Before Implementation

Every system must define its primary responsibility, boundaries and relationships before implementation begins.

# Constitutional Decision Rules

Every constitutional decision must follow:

1. Identify the proposed change.
2. State the problem or contradiction.
3. Gather supporting evidence.
4. Evaluate alternative interpretations.
5. Identify affected systems and relationships.
6. Assess risk and migration impact.
7. Record the proposed decision.
8. Review against constitutional laws.
9. Approve or reject explicitly.
10. Preserve the decision history.

No constitutional change may be adopted through silent editing.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following conditions must remain true unless formally amended:

- Reality remains the final authority.
- Every system owns one primary responsibility.
- Governance does not silently execute.
- Execution does not silently govern.
- ARC preserves persistent memory.
- WOS preserves live state.
- ARC OS performs controlled execution.
- VOID may recommend but does not gain automatic authority.
- Relationships remain first-class objects.
- Constitutional history remains traceable.
- Meaning remains separate from storage location.
- Every high-impact decision remains auditable.

# Constitutional Violations

A constitutional violation occurs when a system, process or actor:

- Assumes another system’s primary responsibility
- Bypasses required approval
- Hides material evidence
- Rewrites constitutional history silently
- Treats storage location as constitutional identity
- Executes without authority
- Declares truth without sufficient evidence
- Removes required auditability
- Prevents valid feedback
- Violates a constitutional invariant
