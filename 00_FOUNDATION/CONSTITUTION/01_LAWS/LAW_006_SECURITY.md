# LAW 006 — Security

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Ratified  
Authority: HOLY ARC Constitution  
Classification: Internal

---

# Law

> Every constitutional action shall occur within explicit identity, authority, permission and governed trust boundaries.

Technical capability shall never override constitutional authority.

---

# Purpose

Protect the constitutional integrity of the HOLY ARC ecosystem while preserving trust, accountability and continuity.

Security exists to protect:

- Responsibilities
- Evidence
- Truth
- State
- Relationships
- Governance

---

# Derived Rules

## R006.1 — Identity

Every constitutional actor shall possess a verifiable identity.

Actors include:

- People
- Systems
- Services
- AI
- Processes
- External organisations

Anonymous constitutional actions are prohibited.

---

## R006.2 — Authority

Authority shall be explicitly granted.

Authority defines:

- Responsibilities
- Decision rights
- Approval rights
- Observation rights
- Modification rights
- Delegation rights

Authority shall never be assumed.

---

## R006.3 — Least Authority

Every actor shall operate with only the authority required to fulfil its constitutional responsibilities.

Additional authority requires governed approval.

---

## R006.4 — Trust Boundaries

Every constitutional system shall define its trust boundary.

Crossing a trust boundary requires:

- Identity verification
- Authority validation
- Permission validation
- Auditability

Trust shall never be implied.

---

## R006.5 — Permission

Permissions govern individual actions.

Permission shall identify:

- Actor
- Action
- Resource
- Scope
- Duration
- Conditions

Permission without authority is invalid.

Authority without permission does not authorise action.

---

## R006.6 — Separation of Duties

Where constitutional risk is significant:

- Review
- Approval
- Execution

should be separated between different responsible actors whenever practical.

This reduces systemic risk.

---

## R006.7 — Auditability

Security-relevant actions shall produce evidence.

Evidence should record:

- Identity
- Authority
- Permission
- Action
- Time
- Outcome

Security actions shall remain reviewable.

---

## R006.8 — Integrity

Constitutional assets shall be protected against:

- Unauthorised modification
- Unauthorised deletion
- Silent corruption
- Loss of provenance
- Undocumented replacement

Integrity takes precedence over convenience.

---

## R006.9 — Containment

Security failures should remain contained within their trust boundary whenever reasonably achievable.

A compromise of one system shall not automatically compromise constitutional authority.

---

## R006.10 — Emergency Authority

Emergency actions may occur only when:

- Justified
- Recorded
- Time-bounded
- Reviewed afterwards

Emergency authority shall never become permanent authority.

---

# Constitutional Tests

Security complies with LAW 006 when:

- Identity is verifiable.
- Authority is explicit.
- Permissions are governed.
- Trust boundaries are documented.
- Security actions are auditable.
- Constitutional assets maintain integrity.
- Emergency actions remain reviewable.

---

# Violations

The following violate LAW 006:

- Anonymous actions
- Assumed authority
- Excessive privilege
- Hidden permissions
- Undocumented trust relationships
- Unauthorised modification
- Permanent emergency authority
- Security bypassing constitutional governance
