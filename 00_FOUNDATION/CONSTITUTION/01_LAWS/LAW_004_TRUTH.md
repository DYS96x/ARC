# LAW 004 — Truth

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Ratified  
Authority: HOLY ARC Constitution  
Classification: Internal

---

# Law

> Constitutional truth shall be established through governed assessment of evidence and remain open to challenge by reality.

Truth is not determined by authority.

Truth is not determined by popularity.

Truth is established through evidence, assessment and validation.

---

# Purpose

Ensure every constitutional conclusion is:

- Evidence-based
- Explicitly assessed
- Transparent
- Reviewable
- Challengeable
- Capable of evolving when reality changes

---

# Derived Rules

## R004.1 — Evidence Before Truth

Evidence shall exist before constitutional truth is accepted.

Evidence alone does not establish truth.

Evidence must first be assessed.

---

## R004.2 — Truth Classification

Every significant claim shall be classified as one of:

- Confirmed Fact
- Observation
- Pattern
- Hypothesis
- Assumption
- Organisational Statement
- Unknown
- Constitutional Knowledge

Classification shall remain visible.

---

## R004.3 — Confidence

Every significant conclusion shall include confidence.

Confidence shall be explicit.

Confidence may be represented as:

- Low
- Medium
- High

or

- Numerical score
- Probability range

Confidence shall never be hidden.

---

## R004.4 — Assessment

Truth shall be derived through assessment of:

- Evidence quality
- Evidence relevance
- Evidence reliability
- Evidence completeness
- Contradictory evidence
- Context

Assessment shall be reviewable.

---

## R004.5 — Contradiction

Contradictory evidence shall be preserved.

Contradiction shall trigger review.

Contradiction shall never be silently discarded.

---

## R004.6 — Provisional Truth

When evidence is incomplete:

A provisional conclusion may be adopted.

The conclusion shall identify:

- Confidence
- Known evidence
- Missing evidence
- Risk
- Review condition

Provisional truth shall never be represented as permanent fact.

---

## R004.7 — Supersession

When stronger evidence becomes available:

- Preserve previous truth.
- Preserve previous evidence.
- Record new evidence.
- Record why truth changed.
- Update constitutional knowledge.

History remains immutable.

Truth evolves.

---

## R004.8 — Reality

Reality is the final constitutional validator.

Every significant conclusion should define how reality can confirm or invalidate it.

Reality has authority over models.

Reality has authority over assumptions.

Reality has authority over predictions.

---

# Constitutional Tests

Truth complies with LAW 004 when:

- Evidence exists.
- Assessment is documented.
- Confidence is explicit.
- Contradictions are preserved.
- Reality can validate the conclusion.
- History is maintained.

---

# Violations

The following violate LAW 004:

- Declaring certainty without evidence
- Presenting assumptions as facts
- Ignoring contradictory evidence
- Hiding uncertainty
- Rewriting previous truth without history
- Allowing authority to replace evidence

---

# Constitutional Mapping

LAW 004 governs:

- CAF assessments
- ARC reasoning
- ARC knowledge
- HOLY ARC constitutional review
- Organisational decision-making
- Strategic recommendations

---

# Audit Criteria

LAW 004 passes audit when:

- Conclusions are evidence-based.
- Confidence is visible.
- Assessment is documented.
- Contradictions are preserved.
- Reality remains capable of disproving the conclusion.

---

# Final Principle

> Evidence informs. Assessment interprets. Reality decides.
