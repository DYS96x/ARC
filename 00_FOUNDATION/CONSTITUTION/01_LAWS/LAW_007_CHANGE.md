# LAW 007 — Change

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Ratified  
Authority: HOLY ARC Constitution  
Classification: Internal

---

# Law

> Every constitutional change shall be deliberate, evidence-based, traceable, reviewable and governed.

Change strengthens the ecosystem.

Uncontrolled change weakens it.

---

# Purpose

Govern how the HOLY ARC ecosystem evolves while preserving constitutional integrity, institutional memory and long-term trust.

---

# Derived Rules

## R007.1 — Explicit Change

Every significant change shall identify:

- Purpose
- Owner
- Scope
- Reason
- Expected outcome
- Impact

Silent change is prohibited.

---

## R007.2 — Constitutional Alignment

Every change shall remain consistent with:

- Constitution
- Constitutional Laws
- Governance

If conflict exists, the higher constitutional authority prevails.

---

## R007.3 — Evidence

Material change shall be supported by evidence.

Evidence may include:

- Audit findings
- Measurements
- User feedback
- Operational experience
- Architectural review
- Risk analysis

Preference alone shall not justify constitutional change.

---

## R007.4 — Impact Assessment

Significant changes shall assess impacts on:

- Responsibilities
- Relationships
- State
- Security
- Knowledge
- Architecture
- Operations

Known risks shall be documented.

---

## R007.5 — Traceability

Every approved change shall record:

- Identifier
- Owner
- Date
- Version
- Evidence
- Decision
- Outcome

Future reviewers shall understand why the change occurred.

---

## R007.6 — Versioning

Constitutional changes shall be versioned.

Version history shall remain available.

Historical versions shall never be destroyed.

---

## R007.7 — Validation

Every significant change shall define:

- Success criteria
- Validation method
- Review date
- Failure criteria

Implementation alone does not prove success.

---

## R007.8 — Reversibility

Where reasonably practical, changes shall define:

- Rollback strategy
- Recovery process
- Review trigger

Irreversible change requires explicit justification.

---

## R007.9 — Deprecation

Replacement of constitutional capability shall include:

- Successor
- Migration guidance
- Version reference
- Historical preservation

Institutional knowledge shall remain intact.

---

## R007.10 — Continuous Learning

Every completed change shall contribute to constitutional learning.

Lessons learned should improve:

- Standards
- Specifications
- Architecture
- Governance
- Future decisions

Improvement is cumulative.

---

# Constitutional Tests

LAW 007 complies when:

- Change is explicit.
- Ownership is clear.
- Evidence supports the change.
- Impact has been assessed.
- Version history exists.
- Validation is defined.
- Learning is preserved.

---

# Violations

The following violate LAW 007:

- Undocumented change
- Unsupported change
- Breaking constitutional hierarchy
- Missing version history
- Missing validation
- Destroying historical rationale
- Silent behavioural change

---

# Constitutional Mapping

LAW 007 governs:

- Constitutional amendments
- Standards evolution
- Specification updates
- Architectural evolution
- Operational improvement
- Organisational learning

---

# Audit Criteria

LAW 007 passes audit when:

- Every significant change is documented.
- Evidence supports change.
- Version history is preserved.
- Validation occurs.
- Lessons learned remain accessible.

---

# Final Principle

> Change is governed. Learning is preserved. History is never lost.
