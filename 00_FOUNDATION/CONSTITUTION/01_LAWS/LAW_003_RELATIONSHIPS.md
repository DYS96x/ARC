# LAW 003 — Relationships

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Ratified  
Authority: HOLY ARC Constitution  
Classification: Internal

---

# Law

> Every interaction within the HOLY ARC ecosystem shall occur through explicit, governed and auditable relationships.

Relationships are constitutional assets.

No constitutional interaction shall exist without a defined relationship.

---

# Purpose

Define how constitutional systems communicate, collaborate and exchange information while preserving ownership, accountability and constitutional integrity.

---

# Derived Rules

## R003.1 — Explicit Relationships

Every relationship shall define:

- Source
- Destination
- Purpose
- Constitutional owner
- Scope
- Lifecycle

Relationships shall never be implied.

---

## R003.2 — Controlled Flow

Every relationship shall define:

- Permitted information
- Restricted information
- Direction of communication
- Trigger conditions
- Expected outcomes

Undefined communication is prohibited.

---

## R003.3 — Constitutional Ownership

Every relationship shall have exactly one accountable owner.

The relationship owner is responsible for:

- Integrity
- Availability
- Evolution
- Compliance
- Auditability

Relationship ownership does not transfer responsibility between participating systems.

---

## R003.4 — Evidence

Significant relationship activity shall produce evidence where appropriate.

Evidence should identify:

- Source
- Destination
- Owner
- Time
- Context
- Outcome

Relationship activity shall remain reviewable.

---

## R003.5 — Boundary Protection

Relationships shall not:

- Circumvent constitutional ownership
- Escalate authority
- Bypass governance
- Rewrite responsibilities
- Modify constitutional truth

Relationships enable cooperation.

They do not redefine constitutional authority.

---

## R003.6 — Versioning

Relationship changes shall be governed.

Breaking changes require:

- Review
- Approval
- Documentation
- Version increment
- Impact assessment

Historical compatibility shall be preserved where practical.

---

## R003.7 — Lifecycle

Every relationship shall progress through:

- Proposed
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Active
- Modified
- Deprecated
- Retired

Relationships outside this lifecycle are unconstitutional.

---

## R003.8 — Failure Handling

Every relationship shall define:

- Failure conditions
- Recovery behaviour
- Escalation path
- Evidence requirements
- Review process

Failure shall never silently violate constitutional integrity.

---

# Constitutional Tests

A relationship complies with LAW 003 when:

- Purpose is defined.
- Participants are identified.
- Ownership is explicit.
- Information flow is governed.
- Evidence is attributable.
- Lifecycle is documented.
- Failure handling is defined.
- Constitutional boundaries are preserved.

---

# Violations

The following violate LAW 003:

- Hidden communication
- Undefined dependencies
- Relationships without ownership
- Unauthorised information flow
- Silent interface changes
- Bypassing governance
- Relationships that cannot be audited
