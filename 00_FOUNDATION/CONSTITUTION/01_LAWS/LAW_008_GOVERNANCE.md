# LAW 008 — Governance

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Ratified  
Authority: HOLY ARC Constitution  
Classification: Internal

---

# Law

> All constitutional authority shall be exercised through explicit governance, accountable ownership, evidence-based decisions and transparent review.

Governance protects the integrity of the ecosystem.

---

# Purpose

Define how authority is exercised, decisions are made, conflicts are resolved and constitutional evolution is controlled.

Governance creates trust between systems, people and decisions.

---

# Derived Rules

## R008.1 — Constitutional Authority

The Constitution is the highest authority within the HOLY ARC ecosystem.

All layers derive authority from:

1. Constitution
2. Laws
3. Standards
4. Specifications
5. Contracts
6. Architecture
7. Engineering
8. Operations

Lower layers cannot override higher layers.

---

## R008.2 — Decision Ownership

Every significant decision shall identify:

- Decision owner
- Supporting evidence
- Decision scope
- Authority basis
- Expected outcome
- Review condition

A decision without ownership is incomplete.

---

## R008.3 — Evidence-Based Decisions

Decisions shall be supported by:

- Relevant evidence
- Context
- Known risks
- Alternatives considered
- Expected impact

Authority may approve a decision.

Authority cannot replace evidence.

---

## R008.4 — Review

Significant decisions shall be reviewed against:

- Constitutional alignment
- Laws
- Responsibilities
- Relationships
- Security
- Long-term impact

Review strengthens decisions.

---

## R008.5 — Challenge

Constitutional decisions may be challenged.

A valid challenge must provide:

- Decision being challenged
- Evidence
- Constitutional basis
- Proposed resolution

Good-faith challenge strengthens governance.

---

## R008.6 — Ratification

A decision becomes active only after appropriate approval.

Ratification shall preserve:

- Decision record
- Owner
- Evidence
- Date
- Version
- Scope

---

## R008.7 — Amendment

Constitutional amendments require:

- Evidence
- Review
- Approval
- Version update
- Historical preservation

Previous versions shall remain accessible.

---

## R008.8 — Conflict Resolution

When conflicts occur:

1. Preserve evidence.
2. Identify conflicting responsibilities.
3. Review constitutional hierarchy.
4. Determine lawful authority.
5. Resolve conflict.
6. Record the outcome.

Personal preference shall not override constitutional authority.

---

## R008.9 — Accountability After Approval

Approval does not remove responsibility.

Decision owners remain responsible for:

- Monitoring outcomes
- Reviewing evidence
- Identifying failure
- Initiating correction where required

---

## R008.10 — Governance Integrity

Governance shall preserve:

- Trust
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Adaptability
- Historical continuity

Governance exists to strengthen the ecosystem, not create unnecessary control.

---

# Constitutional Tests

LAW 008 complies when:

- Authority is explicit.
- Decisions have owners.
- Evidence supports decisions.
- Review exists.
- Challenges are permitted.
- Amendments preserve history.
- Conflicts resolve through hierarchy.
- Accountability remains after approval.

---

# Violations

The following violate LAW 008:

- Undefined authority
- Decisions without owners
- Decisions without evidence
- Bypassing constitutional hierarchy
- Preventing legitimate challenge
- Silent amendments
- Erasing governance history
- Approval without accountability
