# LAW 002 — Evidence

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Ratified  
Authority: HOLY ARC Constitution  
Classification: Internal

---

# Law

> Every significant constitutional decision, observation, recommendation, approval, rejection, and change shall be supported by attributable evidence.

Evidence is the constitutional foundation of truth.

---

# Purpose

Ensure every constitutional decision can be understood, justified, audited, challenged and reproduced.

Evidence establishes confidence.

Evidence does not automatically establish truth.

---

# Derived Rules

## R002.1 — Evidence Required

The following shall produce evidence:

- Decisions
- Assessments
- Recommendations
- Approvals
- Rejections
- Changes
- Reviews
- Exceptions
- Constitutional amendments

---

## R002.2 — Attribution

Every evidence record shall identify:

- Source
- Time
- Responsible owner
- Context
- Related responsibility
- Related relationship

Evidence without attribution shall not establish constitutional truth.

---

## R002.3 — Traceability

Evidence shall remain traceable from:

Reality

↓

Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Assessment

↓

Decision

↓

Outcome

↓

Learning

Future reviewers shall be able to reconstruct the reasoning behind every significant decision.

---

## R002.4 — Integrity

Evidence shall preserve:

- Authenticity
- Accuracy
- Provenance
- Completeness

Evidence shall never be silently modified.

Corrections require preserved history.

---

## R002.5 — Reviewability

Evidence shall remain reviewable.

Evidence may be challenged.

Challenges shall themselves produce evidence.

Review strengthens constitutional confidence.

---

## R002.6 — Reproducibility

Where practical, evidence should enable another reviewer to reproduce the assessment using:

- The same inputs
- The same method
- The same context

---

## R002.7 — Preservation

Evidence supporting constitutional decisions shall be retained according to governance policy.

Deletion of required evidence constitutes a constitutional violation.

---

## R002.8 — Supersession

When stronger evidence exists:

- Preserve previous evidence.
- Preserve previous conclusions.
- Record the new evidence.
- Record the reason for change.
- Update constitutional truth.

History remains immutable.

Truth may evolve.

---

# Constitutional Tests

Evidence complies with LAW 002 when:

- It is attributable.
- It is relevant.
- It is traceable.
- It is reviewable.
- It is preserved.
- It supports the decision being made.
- It has not been silently altered.

---

# Violations

The following violate LAW 002:

- Decisions without evidence
- Anonymous evidence
- Destroying required evidence
- Altering evidence without history
- Treating unsupported claims as constitutional truth
- Ignoring stronger evidence without justification

---

# Constitutional Mapping

LAW 002 governs:

- Constitutional decisions
- Organisational assessments
- Architectural reviews
- Operational changes
- Knowledge creation
- Historical preservation
- Constitutional amendments

---

# Audit Criteria

LAW 002 passes audit when:

- Every significant decision has supporting evidence.
- Evidence is attributable.
- Evidence remains reviewable.
- History is preserved.
- Stronger evidence updates constitutional understanding without erasing previous records.

---

# Final Principle

> Evidence preserves trust. Truth is earned through evidence, never assumed through authority.
