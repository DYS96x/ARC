## Design Philosophy

Everything meaningful exists inside a system.

Every system has:

- Participants
- Relationships
- Signals
- Decisions
- Actions
- Memory
- Feedback
- Consequences
- Evolution

ARC does not treat systems as one-way pipelines.

Every meaningful relationship should support appropriate feedback.

Systems must remain:

- Bidirectional
- Recursive
- Observable
- Correctable
- Secure
- Adaptable
