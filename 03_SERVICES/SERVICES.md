# ARC Services

## Philosophy

ARC does not sell hours.

# Service 01 — ARC Pulse™

## Purpose

Measure the health of an organisation.

## Ideal Client

Leaders who know something is wrong but don't yet know why.

## Deliverables

- Executive interviews
- Systems health assessment
- Process review
- Risk summary
- Opportunity map
- Executive report

## Outcome

A clear picture of the organisation's current reality.
