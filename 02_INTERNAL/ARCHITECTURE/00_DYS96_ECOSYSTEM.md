## Canonical Responsibilities

### DYS96 Ecosystem

The complete ecosystem, identity, portfolio and long-term architecture.

### 6AME 9ESIGN

Systems Studio.

Owns:

- Vision
- Strategy
- Clients
- Revenue
- Partnerships
- Systems design
- Product portfolio
- Commercial delivery

### VOID

Intelligence Operating System.

Owns:

- Observation
- Discovery
- Memory
- Reasoning
- Pattern recognition
- Prediction
- Recommendation
- Explanation
- Planning
- Learning

### HOLY ARC

Platform Operating System.

Connects business intent and intelligence to reusable platform capabilities.

Provides access to:

- WOS
- Services
- World Engine
- Platform APIs
- VOID intelligence

### WOS

Coordination system.

Owns:

- Workflows
- Orchestration
- Scheduling
- Tasks
- Events
- Approvals
- Permissions
- Automation
- Escalation

### Services

Execution system.

Owns:

- Reports
- Documents
- Export
- Notifications
- Integrations
- Publishing
- Deployment
- Operational automation

### World Engine

Simulation and context system.

Models:

- Organisations
- Users
- Geography
- Economies

- Relationships
- Signals
- Consequences
- Digital twins
- Possible future states

Integration system.

Provides controlled and versioned access between:

- Experiences

- External integrations
- AI agents
- Products
- Platform services

### Experience Layer

Where users receive value.

Includes:

- FACEXOFF

- Partner applications

## Canonical Responsibility Law

6AME 9ESIGN designs.

VOID thinks.

HOLY ARC enables.

WOS coordinates.

Services execute.

World Engine simulates context.

Platform APIs integrate.

Experiences deliver value.

---

## Architectural Laws

1. Each layer has one primary responsibility.
2. Experiences express intent but do not control platform implementation.
3. VOID may recommend but does not bypass governance.
4. Shared capabilities belong in HOLY ARC.
5. Product-specific logic remains in the experience.
6. Every meaningful relationship supports appropriate feedback.
7. Products strengthen the platform.
8. Reality remains the final authority.
