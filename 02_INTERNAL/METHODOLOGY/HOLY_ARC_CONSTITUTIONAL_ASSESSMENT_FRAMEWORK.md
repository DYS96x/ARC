# Framework Definition

> The HOLY ARC Constitutional Assessment Framework evaluates the alignment between an organisation's intended future, current reality, operating systems and capability to evolve.

---

# Purpose

The framework exists to understand organisations before recommending change.

It answers:

- What future is this organisation creating?
- Why does this organisation exist?
- What systems currently support or restrict that future?
- What capabilities must evolve?

# Core Principles

## Reality Before Recommendation

Assessment begins with understanding reality.

---

## Evidence Before Assumption

Observations must be separated from conclusions.

---

## Systems Before Symptoms

Problems are investigated through the systems that create them.

---

## Capability Before Activity

Progress is measured by increased capability, not simply increased activity.

---

## Long-Term Evolution Before Short-Term Optimisation

Solutions must improve future ability.

# Stage 1 — Vision Assessment

Purpose:

Understand the future the organisation is attempting to create.

Questions:

- What future does the organisation believe is possible?
- What change does it want to create?
- What does success look like?
- Why should this organisation exist?

Output:

```text
Vision Map
```

---

# Stage 2 — Constitutional Alignment

Evaluate:

## Purpose

Why does the organisation exist?

## Identity

What makes it unique?

## Principles

What guides decisions?

## Values

What behaviours are protected?

Output:

```text
Constitutional Alignment Report
```

---

# Stage 3 — Reality Assessment

Compare:

```text
Declared Reality

        VS

Operational Reality
```

Evaluate:

- Decision making
- Communication
- Leadership
- Culture
- Processes
- Resources
- Constraints

Output:

```text
Reality Map
```

## Operational System

## Technology System

## Data System
