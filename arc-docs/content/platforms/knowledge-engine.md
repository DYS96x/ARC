# Knowledge Engine

## Purpose

The Knowledge Engine transforms information into organisational intelligence.

Knowledge is not simply stored.

It is connected.

Versioned.

Searchable.

Traceable.

Continuously improved.

Knowledge should compound over time.

---

# Core Principle

Information becomes knowledge when it gains context.

Knowledge becomes intelligence when relationships become visible.

---

# Knowledge Lifecycle

Capture

↓

↓

Validate

↓

Version

↓

Discover

↓

↓

Improve

Nothing valuable should end at storage.

---

# Relationships

Knowledge should answer:

What relates to this?

What depends on this?

What created this?

What changed this?

What replaced this?

What references this?

Everything exists inside a connected graph.

---

# Version History

Knowledge should never be overwritten.

Every revision records:

Who changed it

When

Why

What changed

Approval (where required)

History becomes organisational memory.

---

# Search

Search should support:

Keywords

Natural language

Semantic similarity

Tags

Metadata

Relationships

Ownership

Date ranges

Security permissions

The best answer should be discoverable.

---

# Validation

Not all knowledge has equal confidence.

Each item should have:

Draft

Under Review

Validated

Approved

Archived

Confidence should always be visible.

---

# Governance

Every knowledge object has:

Owner

Reviewer

Approval path

Retention policy

Archive policy

Security classification

Knowledge without ownership decays.
