# ARC Client Journey

## Purpose

Every client engagement follows a consistent journey.

The objective is to reduce uncertainty, build trust, and create measurable value at every stage.

Clients should always know:

- Where they are
- Why they are there
- What happens next
- What success looks like

---

# Stage 01 — Initial Contact

## Goal

Understand why the client reached out.

## Activities

- Introductory conversation
- High-level objectives
- Current challenges
- Desired outcomes
- Fit assessment

## Success

Both ARC and the client understand whether a partnership is appropriate.

---

# Stage 02 — ARC Pulse™

## Goal

Establish the current reality.

## Activities

- Interviews
- Process review
- Systems assessment
- Technology review
- Risk identification

## Deliverables

- Current State Report
- Key Findings
- Initial Recommendations

---

# Stage 03 — ARC Discovery™

## Goal

Understand the organisation beneath the surface.

## Activities

- Workflow mapping
- Decision mapping
- Information flow analysis
- Organisational observations
- Stakeholder workshops

## Deliverables

- Reality Report
- Opportunity Map
- Discovery Summary

---

# Stage 04 — ARC Blueprint™

## Deliverables

- Operating System Blueprint

- Executive Presentation

# Success Metrics

A successful engagement results in:

- Better decisions
- Better systems
- Better documentation
- Better collaboration
- Better security
- Better organisational capability

# ARC Commitment

Every engagement should leave the client stronger than when they arrived.

Success is measured by the client's ability to operate independently with greater confidence, clarity, and capability.
