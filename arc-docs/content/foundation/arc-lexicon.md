# ARC Lexicon

## Purpose

The ARC Lexicon defines the official language of ARC.

Words shape thinking.

Thinking shapes systems.

Every important concept should have one agreed meaning.

If two people use the same word differently, the system becomes inconsistent.

The Lexicon exists to eliminate ambiguity.

---

# System

A collection of connected parts working together toward a purpose.

A system is never just software.

People, processes, knowledge, technology and feedback all form part of the system.

---

# Reality

The observable behaviour of the system.

Reality always overrides assumptions.

---

# Truth

A statement supported by repeatable evidence.

Truth is earned.

Opinion is not truth.

---

# Observation

The unbiased recording of reality.

Observation happens before analysis.

---

# Discovery

The process of revealing relationships that were previously unknown.

---

# Relationship

A meaningful connection between two or more things.

Relationships create systems.

---

# Signal

A meaningful piece of information indicating change.

Signals reduce uncertainty.

Noise increases uncertainty.

---

# Knowledge

Validated information with context.

Knowledge explains.

---

# Intelligence

The ability to reason using knowledge.

Intelligence recommends.

Humans decide.

---

# Workflow

The movement of work from one state to another.

---

# Decision

A committed choice that changes the future state of the system.

Important decisions require evidence.

---

# Capability

Something the organisation can reliably do.

Capabilities outlive individual people.

---

# Platform

A reusable system that enables multiple experiences.

---

# Product

A packaged capability delivering measurable value.

---

# Experience

How humans interact with a system.

Good systems create simple experiences.

---

# Architecture

The intentional organisation of systems and relationships.

Architecture defines behaviour.

Technology implements architecture.

---

# Feedback

Information returned by reality after action.

Feedback enables learning.

---

# Learning

The improvement of future decisions using validated feedback.

---

# Multiplier

Anything that increases future capability.

The multiplier is ARC's preferred measure of long-term progress.

---

# Principle

A rule that guides decisions across many situations.

Principles change rarely.

Processes evolve often.

---

# Engine

A reusable capability with a clearly defined responsibility.

Engines should never overlap ownership.

---

# Contract

A formal agreement between systems.

Contracts create predictable behaviour.

---

# Governance

The framework for ownership, accountability and decision making.

---

# Final Principle

If a new term becomes important...

It belongs in the Lexicon.

ARC should speak one language.
