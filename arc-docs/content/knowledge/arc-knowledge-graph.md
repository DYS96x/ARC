# ARC Knowledge Graph

## Purpose

The ARC Knowledge Graph is the living intelligence layer of ARC.

It connects every idea, document, decision, project, person, platform, workflow, client, methodology, principle, and outcome into one continuously evolving system.

Documents store information.

Graphs store understanding.

The purpose is not to remember everything.

The purpose is to understand relationships.

---

# First Principle

Nothing exists alone.

Everything has relationships.

Understanding relationships creates intelligence.

---

# Core Philosophy

Traditional documentation asks:

"What is this?"

The Knowledge Graph asks:

"What is this connected to?"

Understanding emerges through relationships.
