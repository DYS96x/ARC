# ARC Business Model

## Purpose

ARC exists to design systems that help organisations become more intelligent, efficient, secure, and scalable.

We are a Systems Studio.

We do not sell software.

We do not sell AI.

We design systems.

---

# What We Sell

ARC sells:

- Clarity
- System Design
- Business Operating Systems
- Intelligence Architecture
- Knowledge Architecture
- Workflow Design
- Platform Strategy
- Long-Term Capability

Technology is the implementation.

The system is the product.
