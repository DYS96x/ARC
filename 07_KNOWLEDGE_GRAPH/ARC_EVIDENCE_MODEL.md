# ARC Evidence Model

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Foundation

Authority:

ARC Knowledge Graph

---

# Definition

Evidence represents information that supports, challenges, or explains relationships within the ARC knowledge graph.

Evidence allows ARC to distinguish assumptions from validated understanding.

---

# Core Evidence Principle

Information is not automatically knowledge.

Evidence gives information context, traceability, and confidence.

```text
Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Validation

↓

Knowledge
