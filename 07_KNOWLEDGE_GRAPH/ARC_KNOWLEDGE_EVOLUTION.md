# Definition

Knowledge evolution defines how ARC transforms validated information into improved understanding, principles, decisions, and system capability.

Knowledge is not static storage.

Knowledge is a continuously evolving relationship between evidence, patterns, reality, and improvement.

---

# Core Knowledge Principle

Information becomes knowledge through validation.

Knowledge becomes intelligence through application.

Knowledge becomes wisdom through repeated reality validation.
