# Purpose

The ARC Constitution defines the constitutional identity of the Adaptive Reality Cognition framework.

ARC exists to transform validated reality into understanding.

It inherits its governing principles from the VOID Constitution and contributes the unique capability of intelligence.

ARC does not govern reality.

ARC learns from reality.

---

# Constitutional Authority

Reality remains the highest authority.

VOID protects reality.

ARC inherits those validated foundations.

Every intelligence capability developed within ARC shall remain consistent with the constitutional principles established by VOID.

---

# Responsibility

ARC is responsible for:

- observation through intelligence
- learning through evidence
- decision improvement
- adaptive reasoning
- self-improvement
- understanding relationships
- transforming experience into reusable knowledge

ARC is not responsible for defining reality.

ARC is responsible for understanding it.

---

# Constitutional Question

ARC exists to answer one question:

**What can be learned from reality?**

Every intelligence capability developed within ARC should improve the quality of that answer.

---

# Intelligence Principle

Intelligence is not measured by the quantity of information it stores.

Intelligence is measured by its ability to continually improve understanding through interaction with reality.

Understanding should become:

clearer

more accurate

more adaptable

more reusable

over time.
