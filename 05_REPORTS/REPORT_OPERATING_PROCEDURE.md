## Rules

1. Every report receives a unique Report ID.

2. Reports begin from the approved ARC report template.

3. Client facts and ARC analysis must remain clearly separated.

4. Sources must be recorded beneath the claims they support.

5. Sensitive client reports must never enter 00_PUBLIC.

6. Only approved reports may move into 06_EXPORTS.

7. Only the final sent version moves into 07_SENT.

8. Supporting evidence remains in the client's private workspace.

9. Every sent report records its recipient and sent date.

10. Lessons learned return to ARC internal knowledge without exposing client information.
